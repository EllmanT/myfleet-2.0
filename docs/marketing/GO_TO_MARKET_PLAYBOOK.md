# myFleet Go-To-Market Playbook

Written 3 Nov 2026 for the owner (Allea Worldwide Logistics), working alone with a phone and a laptop.
Goal: free-tier sign-ups from Zimbabwean cross-border owner-operators and small fleets (2-20 trucks) before year end, so they can put myFleet in their 2027 budgets.

## 0. Ground rules and owner decisions

**Labels used.** *Verified* = found in a linked source. *Assumption* = a planning number to test, not a fact. *Unverified* = could not confirm.

**Owner decisions this playbook is built on**
- Launch prices are a **test**: **$9 / $29 / $59 per month** for **1-2 / 3-10 / 11-25 trucks**. Revisit after the first 10-15 operator conversations (matches `docs/SAAS_PLAN.md`). Every post and message that shows a price must say "launch price, may change before you pay" and the currency (USD).
- Free first month for solo operators, 2-3 months for fleets, disclosed up front, no card. Suggested split to decide before launch: 1-2 trucks = 1 month, 3-10 = 2 months, 11-25 = 3 months. Payment is manual (EcoCash or bank transfer), never automatic.
- The landing page is a **separate static marketing site**. Its sign-up buttons go to the app and to WhatsApp (wa.me).
- **Screenshots come from a demo company with invented data. Never real customer data**, including Allea's. Caption them "demo data".
- Allea Worldwide Logistics is the owner's own company. It is the live case study, but always **disclose that**: "our own fleet business". Case-study claims are words and only true, reproducible facts. Screenshots stay demo.
- **No published AI accuracy figures until measured** on real logbooks, with a kept test log. Until then use the honest wording in section 8.
- Legal notes: `docs/research/legal-and-compliance-notes.md` (research, not legal advice). Section 8 applies them.

**What I could not verify.** I found **no** Zimbabwean trucking Facebook group, WhatsApp community or Telegram channel by name, member count or admin. Public search does not index them. Section 1 gives a method to find real ones. Do not paste any group name from anyone, including an LLM, until you have opened and checked it. I invented no statistics. Funnel numbers in 7.3 are labelled assumptions.

**Dates.** Pre-launch week 3-8 Nov. Week 1 starts Mon 9 Nov. Week 6 ends Sun 20 Dec 2026.

### Strategy: Hormozi's Core Four, applied

Third-party summaries of *$100M Leads* describe the Core Four as warm outreach, posting content, cold outreach and paid ads, suggest starting with the lowest-friction one (warm), and describe "lead getters" (referrers, affiliates) as a way to multiply reach ([Shortform](https://www.shortform.com/summary/100m-leads-summary-alex-hormozi), [Blinkist](https://www.blinkist.com/books/100m-leads-en), [Alan Spicer](https://alanspicer.com/100m-leads-review-and-summary/)). These are summaries, not the book.

| Core Four | Your version | When |
|---|---|---|
| Warm outreach | Everyone in your phone and Allea's network: transporters, drivers, clearing agents, mechanics | Week 0-1, first. Best conversion per hour (assumption) |
| Post content | 3 posts a week in vetted groups and your own page | Main engine, weeks 1-6 |
| Cold outreach | Only to people who commented or asked. No lists, no bulk DMs | Light, opt-in |
| Paid ads | Click-to-WhatsApp, tiny budget | Optional test in week 5-6, only if organic works |
| Lead getters | Referral reward and partners (section 6) | From week 2 |

The one idea worth using everywhere: give the result before the sale. Your offer is "send me one logbook photo, get an invoice back".

### 1.2 Method to find real communities (about 90 minutes, week 0)

1. **Ask people first.** Message 10 drivers, dispatchers and owners you know: "Which WhatsApp or Facebook groups do you use for loads, border updates, spares?" Real groups come from real people. This is also warm outreach.
2. **Facebook search**, Groups filter: `Zimbabwe transporters`, `Zim truckers`, `cross border Zimbabwe trucks`, `Beitbridge trucks`, `Beira corridor`, `Forbes Machipanda`, `loads Zimbabwe`, `truck for hire Zimbabwe`, `haulage Zimbabwe`, `SADC transporters`, `Chirundu trucks`, `Mutare transporters`.
3. **Google:** `site:facebook.com/groups Zimbabwe transporters` (patchy but sometimes works).
4. **Telegram:** in-app search `loads Zimbabwe`, `Beitbridge`, `Zim transporters`. **LinkedIn:** search Groups and People for "Zimbabwe logistics", "SADC freight". LinkedIn suits clearing agents, forwarders and fleet managers more than owner-operators.
5. **Associations:** look for TOAZ's own site or page; ask a member transporter or clearing agent for a current contact. Treat as unverified until a human member confirms.
6. **Cross-check:** a real group is named by two or more people independently.
7. Log each in the Sheet `Communities` tab (section 7).

### 1.3 Vetting checklist (join only if 7 or more of 10)

1. Members are mostly Zimbabwean/SADC transporters, owners, drivers or dispatchers (check 10 profiles).
2. Posts in the last 7 days are about trucks, loads, borders, spares or documents.
3. Several different people comment, not only the admin.
4. Rules are visible (Facebook group "About").
5. The admin is identifiable and replies to messages.
6. No obvious scams (advance-fee loads, crypto spam).
7. Other business posts exist and survive.
8. Not a pure "loads wanted" feed where anything else is ignored.
9. Activity is plausible for the size. You record the member count yourself on the date you check.
10. You can post under your real name, and your profile shows your business.

Aim for 5-8 groups, ranked. Start with the top three.

## 2. Platform rules and etiquette

### 2.1 Facebook groups

Verified: Meta's spam policy prohibits posting at extremely high volume, repetitive content, deceptive or misleading links, and buying or gating engagement ([Meta Spam policy](https://transparency.meta.com/policies/community-standards/spam/)). Each group also has admin rules, which come first.

Secondary sources (blogs, not Meta; treat as practical norms): promotion is often banned or limited to set days or pinned threads; admins can approve every post; the same link in several groups within an hour can look like spam; words like "free", "DM me" or a bare URL can hold a post ([Lilach Bullock](https://www.lilachbullock.com/stop-facebook-groups-removing-business-posts/), [GroupBoss](https://groupboss.io/blog/facebook-group-rules/amp/)).

Your rules:
- Read the rules before every first post. Obey them over this playbook.
- **Ask the admin first** (script below). Keep a screenshot of the yes in your Sheet.
- Disclose: "I built this. I also run Allea Worldwide Logistics, my own fleet business."
- Never post identical text in several groups on the same day. Change the opening and screenshot.
- Max one promotional post per group per week unless the admin invites more. Answer every comment within 24 hours.
- Don't tag people who didn't ask. Don't add people to groups. Don't DM everyone who reacted.
- Put the wa.me link in the first comment, or the post if allowed. If links get held, ask people to comment "INFO" and reply.
- Always state terms in the same post: first month free, then launch price from $9, no card.
- No "comment YES to win" or like-for-access tricks. That is engagement abuse under Meta's policy.
- Space out DMs and personalise them. No bulk identical DMs.
- If a post is removed, don't repost. Ask the admin what was wrong.
- Keep your own page as a fallback in case a group removes you.

**Admin permission script**
> Hello [Name]. I run Allea Worldwide Logistics, my own fleet business, and I built a simple tool for our back office called myFleet. You take a photo of the driver's logbook and it makes the trip record and an invoice, and it shows who owes you money and which permits are about to expire. Other transporters keep asking me about it. Would it be OK to share one short post with screenshots (demo data) in the group? I will say clearly that I built it and I won't DM anyone who doesn't ask. If you prefer a certain day or a pinned thread, tell me and I'll follow it. Happy to give you a free account to look at first. Thank you.

**If no:** "Understood, thanks for being straight. I'll just answer questions when someone asks about invoices or border paperwork." Then only help. Value comments earn a later yes.
**If yes with conditions:** repeat them back in writing and follow them exactly.

### 2.2 WhatsApp groups

- Join as a person. Introduce yourself once, listen for a week, answer questions without pitching.
- Never add people to groups or lists without agreement. Don't forward unsolicited messages.
- Post only if the admin is fine with it: at most one message a week, text plus one image.
- Move interested people to a **private chat**.
- If someone says stop, stop, and add them to `Do not contact`.

## 3. The funnel and scripts

```
Post -> comment / DM -> WhatsApp chat -> free setup (1 logbook photo -> 1 invoice)
 -> sign-up -> day-1 aha -> day-7 check-in -> day-30 check-in -> convert at end of free period
```

Voice: plain, direct, short, local. Same content in Shona or Ndebele if the person switches. Fill `[ ]` fields. Prices are a test: always add "launch price".

### 3.1 Post
Each post ends with one ask only: comment "INFO" or tap the link.

### 3.2 Comment and DM

**Public reply**
> Thanks [Name]. I'll message you the details on WhatsApp in a minute. If you'd rather I didn't, just say so here.

**DM (only to people who commented INFO or asked)**
> Hi [Name], [Your name] here. You asked about myFleet on the [group] post. So I show you the right thing: how many trucks do you run, and mostly Beitbridge or Beira? If you like, send me one photo of a driver's logbook page and I'll send you back a ready invoice, free. Or chat here: [wa.me link]

No reply after 3 days, one nudge only:
> No pressure [Name]. If paperwork isn't a headache right now, ignore this. The offer to turn one logbook photo into an invoice stands. Reply STOP and I won't message again.

> Hi [Name], thank you for messaging. I'm [Your name]. I built myFleet for my own trucks (Allea Worldwide Logistics, my own fleet business) and I'm now offering it to other transporters. Two quick questions: how many trucks do you run, and how do you do trip records and invoices now (paper, Excel, something else)?

Label `New`. Reply in working hours, within a few hours.

### 3.4 Free setup / demo

> The offer, no catch: send me one photo of a logbook page (up to 3). I'll turn it into trip records and a draft invoice and send it back here, usually within [24 hours]. If you like it, I'll set up your account: first [month / 2 months / 3 months] free, no card. After that it's the launch price of [$9 / $29 / $59] a month for [1-2 / 3-10 / 11-25] trucks. Wait for a yes before processing. Record it (`consent_to_message`, `demo_consent`).

Return of the invoice:

This is a business document, not a ZIMRA fiscalised tax invoice (if you must issue fiscal invoices, you still use your fiscal device). Want me to set up your account? It takes about 10 minutes.

### 3.5 Sign-up (people can sign up themselves)
> Great. Sign up here: [app link with ?utm_source=whatsapp&utm_medium=chat&utm_campaign=launch-nov26]. Use your own email. When you're in, tell me and I'll check your first trip and invoice show up. Stuck? Send a screenshot.

No self-serve in 24 hours: "Want me to do it with you on a 10-minute call?"

### 3.6 Day-1 aha
The aha: **their own trip becomes a trip record and an invoice they can send.**
> Welcome [Name]. Today's one job: upload one logbook photo in photo import, check the rows (fix anything wrong), and save. Then open Invoices, and "Who owes me" will show what your customer owes. Send me a screenshot when it works, or tell me where it stopped.

Log `aha_date` when one photo import and one invoice exist.

### 3.7 Day 7
> One week in, [Name]. Three questions: (1) did you import more than one trip? (2) Did "Who owes me" show anything you didn't already know? (3) What annoyed you? Be blunt. I read every answer myself.

Stalled: "Send me 3 more logbook photos and I'll load them for you this once." Also ask: opt-in to the Friday update list, and "Do you know one other transporter who'd want this?" (referral).

### 3.8 Day 30 (fleets also Day 60)
> You've been on myFleet a month. From your account I can see [N] trips and [N] invoices. [Only say this if the Terms/Privacy notice tells users staff can see usage; otherwise ask them instead.] Did a customer pay faster, or did a document warning help? Be honest and I'll act on it.
> Your free period ends on [date]. After that it's the launch price of [$X] a month. To continue, reply YES and I'll send EcoCash/bank details. If not, no problem. You can export your data first.

### 3.9 Conversion
Three days before the end:
> [Name], your free period ends on [date]. To keep going, pay [$X] by EcoCash to [number, name] or bank transfer to [details], and send me the confirmation. If you don't want to continue, say so and I'll help you export your data. Your account goes read-only for [30] days, then your data is deleted as in the Privacy Policy.

On payment:
> Received, thank you. You're paid until [date]. As a founding member your price is locked at [$X] for [period; decide before you say it].

Never charge automatically. State who bears EcoCash or bank fees.

### 3.10 Objection replies

**Price**
> Fair. Can I ask what a late or unpaid trip invoice costs you? The first [month/2-3 months] is free, so you can see if it helps. If it doesn't, export your data and pay nothing. The launch price is [$9/$29/$59] a month and I'm testing it, so tell me if it's wrong for your size. (Don't promise it pays for itself unless you can show a real case.)

**"I use Excel" / "I use QuickBooks"**
> Excel is fine for numbers. The slow part is typing the driver's paper logbook into it. myFleet reads the photo into rows, then makes the trip, the invoice and "who owes me" together. You can export your data any time. Keep QuickBooks for accounts if you like. [Only mention export formats the app really supports. Check before promising QuickBooks anything.]

**"My drivers won't use it"**
> They don't need to. The driver keeps writing in the same logbook. You or your office photographs the page and checks the rows. If someone can send a photo on WhatsApp, that's enough.

**"Is my data safe?"**
> Right to ask. Your company's data sits in your own account and other companies can't see it. You log in with a password, you can export everything, and you can ask me to delete it. The AI step sends logbook photos to Anthropic (USA) to read the handwriting. Their commercial terms say they don't train on it and delete it from their systems in about 30 days [re-check before saying]. Other providers: [list the subprocessors you actually use: Clerk, Vercel, MongoDB Atlas]. I can't promise nothing can ever go wrong, nobody honest can. If something happens I'll tell you within the legal time. (Say only what is true in the live build.)

**"Will it read my handwriting?"**
> Often it reads well, and sometimes it doesn't. I'd rather you test it than take my word. Send me your worst page. Anything it isn't sure about is marked, and you check and fix the rows before they're saved. Nothing goes on an invoice until you approve it. (No percentage until measured, section 8.4.)

**"I'll think about it"**
> No problem. What would make it an easy yes: price, trust, time, or seeing it work on your own page? If it's the last one, send me a photo.

**"Who are you?"**
> I run Allea Worldwide Logistics, my own fleet business, and I built myFleet for our own trucks. [Add truck count, years, a mutual contact, or your page.] I can show you our routine on a quick video call.

**"Do you take a cut of my payments?"**
> No. You invoice your customers and they pay you. myFleet only records it.

**"Is it a ZIMRA tax invoice?"**
> No. It's a business document. If you must fiscalise, you still issue the fiscal invoice through your fiscal device or system. I'm looking at what to do about that.

**"Does it work on my phone / weak data?"** Answer only after testing on a basic Android. If untested: "I'll check and tell you tomorrow."

## 5. Lead magnets and the "send me one logbook photo" demo

### 5.1 Lead magnets
1. **"Send me one logbook photo, get an invoice back"** (core).
2. **10 questions before you accept a load** (one-page PDF).
3. **Cross-border document expiry tracker** (Google Sheet: truck, document, number, expiry, alert days). Useful alone, leads to the Documents page.
4. Later: trip profit calculator (from SAAS_PLAN).

### 5.2 Running the demo at scale, safely
- **Capacity (assumption):** 5-10 minutes per demo. Cap at 10-15 a day; otherwise say "queue about 2 days".
- **Process:** (1) greet; (2) send the consent message (3.4) and wait for yes; (3) process in a **separate demo workspace**, not mixed with any other customer's data; (4) review every row yourself; (5) send the PDF with uncertain items marked; (6) delete the photo and demo data after 14 days or on request; log it.
- **Limits:** 1-3 pages per person. Logbook pages only: reject ID documents, passports, bank details. Never reuse one person's page in a post or show it to another person. Don't open links people send you and never send or accept money in the demo.
- **Cost:** SAAS_PLAN estimates roughly $0.002 (Haiku) to $0.033 (Sonnet) per page. That is an estimate, not a measurement. Track actual spend in the Costs tab.
- **Promise only what you meet:** "usually within 24 hours".
- **Quality gate:** before the campaign, test 30-50 real pages (SAAS_PLAN already requires this) and keep the log (section 8.4).

### 7.5 Decision rules (adjust after you see data)
- **Kill** a channel or group after 3 posts (2 weeks) if under 3 chats started, or an admin warning/removal, or hours per sign-up exceed double your best channel.
- **Keep** if at least 1 sign-up per 2 posts, or good conversations, and no complaints.
- **Scale** if a channel gets 2x the sign-ups per hour of your next best for two weeks and aha rate is at least 40%. Copy the format into similar groups; consider ads.
- **Fix before pushing:** if sign-ups grow but aha is under 30%, stop outreach and fix onboarding.
- **Pause everything** if three people complain of spam in a week or a group removes you.
- **Ads:** only after 20+ organic chats and a demo-to-sign-up rate you're happy with (suggest 30%+). Run 7 days on a budget you can afford to lose. Kill if cost per chat exceeds your limit.
- **Price test:** after 10-15 conversations, review which band objects to price and whether $9/$29/$59 stays.

## 8. Compliance and honesty

This section applies `docs/research/legal-and-compliance-notes.md`. It is research, not legal advice. Have a Zimbabwean lawyer review before public sign-ups.

### 8.1 Consent for messaging
- Message only people who contacted you, commented asking, or asked you to. Log how and when.
- Separate consents: (a) replying to a request; (b) account check-ins; (c) weekly update broadcasts. Don't bundle (c) into (a).
- STOP line on every broadcast. Honour STOP immediately and log it. WhatsApp's policy requires this and negative reports can limit sending ([policy](https://whatsappbusiness.com/policy/)).
- No bought or scraped lists. No adding people to groups. Never forward one person's chat or invoice to another.
- Zimbabwe's data law also treats direct marketing as processing needing a lawful basis. The exact consent rule is unverified; the lawyer should confirm (legal notes section 7).

### 8.2 Zimbabwe data protection basics
Sources: [Michalsons](https://www.michalsons.com/blog/zimbabwes-cyber-and-data-protection-act-overview/78795), [DLA Piper Africa on SI 155](https://www.dlapiperafrica.com/zimbabwe/insights/2024/A-Quick-Start-Guide-to-Zimbabwes-Data-Protection-Regulations), [MISA Zimbabwe](https://zimbabwe.misa.org/2024/11/09/misa-zimbabwe-analysis-and-position-on-new-data-regulations). Details below are secondary or unverified where stated.
- The Cyber and Data Protection Act (Act 5 of 2021) is enforced by POTRAZ. Under SI 155 of 2024, data controllers need a licence (Form DP1, 12-month term). Fee amounts, tiers, the DPO duty for a tiny business and POTRAZ inspections from Sept 2026 are **unverified**.
- You are likely both a controller (your accounts, billing, marketing lists) and a processor (customer business data). Ask the lawyer.
- Before launch: apply for the POTRAZ licence or get written advice that you don't need one yet; publish a Privacy Policy and Terms with sign-up acceptance; name a privacy contact; keep a breach plan that can meet the reported 24-hour POTRAZ notification (lawyer to confirm); disclose offshore transfers (Anthropic, Clerk, Vercel, MongoDB) and keep their data processing terms on file.
- Minimise data: don't ask for driver national ID numbers unless needed. Keep logbook photos only as long as needed; the notes suggest deleting the original 30-90 days after import and offering a delete button.
- Users must have the right to upload their drivers' and customers' details; the Terms say so.
- Cookies/analytics on the static site: disclose any you use. Marketing lists need opt-in and an unsubscribe route.
- Do not ask for or store EcoCash PINs or card numbers. Give only the account to pay into.

### 8.3 No false claims
- No "saves X%", "faster collection", "most accurate", "used by N companies" unless you have the evidence on file. Avoid "unlimited", "guaranteed", "100%".
- **Allea disclosure:** always say Allea Worldwide Logistics is your own company. Undisclosed self-testimonials risk misleading-advertising rules and Meta policies (legal notes section 7). Allea's results are the owner's own, not independent proof.
- **Testimonials:** get **written** permission (email or WhatsApp text is fine) stating exactly what you'll publish (name, company, quote, numbers, logo) and where. Keep it in the Sheet. Let people withdraw. No invented quotes, ever.
- **Screenshots:** demo company, invented data, captioned "demo data".
- **Scarcity:** only real caps with real counts.
- **Offer terms:** in every offer post: free period length, price is a launch test price in USD, no card, manual payment, what happens at the end (read-only 30 days, export, then deletion per the Privacy Policy). Never auto-charge. State who bears payment fees. Use refund wording specific to the Terms (legal notes section 4), only if you will honour it.
- **Invoices and tax:** myFleet invoices are business documents, **not ZIMRA fiscalised tax invoices**. Say so near the invoice screen, in Terms and in demos. Don't claim ZIMRA compliance ([ZIMRA fiscalisation](https://www.zimra.co.zw/domestic-taxes/corporate/fiscalisation-explained)). State if prices include VAT once you know your VAT position.
- Don't imply endorsement by TOAZ, ZIMRA, POTRAZ, banks, EcoCash, WhatsApp, Meta or Anthropic. Check the "myFleet" name's trademark availability with ZIPO (unverified).
- Only advertise features that exist in the live build.

### 8.4 AI accuracy, honestly
- **Until measured, publish no accuracy number.** Use: "It reads many handwritten logbooks and shows its work. Anything it isn't sure about is marked, and you check and correct every row before it's saved. It can be wrong."
- Never say "100% accurate", "no typing", "reads any handwriting".
- Measure: 30-50 real pages from different drivers, pens, creased paper, night photos. Record per field (date, route, km, load, amount) the share needing correction, with sample size and date. Keep the test log. Only then publish a number, labelled with the sample and date. Re-test when the model or prompt changes.
- Show the review screen in every demo. It's a feature.
- A wrong value that slips through is a bug: tell the affected user and fix it.
- Disclose the AI step where photos are uploaded and in chat: "Photos are sent to Anthropic (USA) via its commercial API to read the handwriting; you confirm before anything is saved." Anthropic's retention (about 30 days; not used for training by default) is documented in the notes but should be re-checked for the model you use ([API retention](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/manage-claude/api-and-data-retention), [commercial retention](https://privacy.claude.com/en/articles/7996866-how-long-do-you-store-my-organization-s-data)).

## 10. Risks
