# Fuel Procedure and Weekly Checklist

Last updated: 13 October 2026

## Why we do this

This routine lets us see where every litre goes, without anyone asking for fuel money each time. It is not about blaming anyone. A clear record protects the drivers as much as the business, because it answers a question before it is asked.

All three trucks together use about 85 litres a week, which is about $175. The numbers below start as estimates and we correct them after the first four Sundays.

## Who does what

Five jobs, each with one name beside it. Fill in the names before the first Sunday.

| Job | Who | What they do |
| --- | --- | --- |

| Cash giver | ____________ | Gives the weekly fuel money on Sunday and writes the amount in the WhatsApp group |

## Sunday fuel routine

Every truck is filled to full once a week, at the same station, on Sunday. Filling to full is what makes the check work, because the litres put in then equal the fuel used since last Sunday.

1. The cash giver posts in the group: "Fuel day. AAV $__, AFO $__, DAF $__". Suggested start amounts are $55 for each Dyna and $90 for the DAF. We adjust after four weeks.
2. Each truck goes to the agreed station: ____________.
3. Fill until the pump clicks off. Do not top up after the click.
4. Take the photos before leaving the pump (next section).
5. Pay at the pump and keep the receipt. Bring any change back to the cash giver the same day.
6. Post the photos and the standard message in the WhatsApp group before leaving the station.
7. The weekly checker enters each fill-up in myfleet, Fuel Log, the same day.

If a truck cannot be filled on Sunday, say so in the group and fill it first thing Monday, with the same rules.

## Photo rules

Every fill needs two photos taken at the pump with the phone camera, not picked from the gallery. Add a third if the pump gives a receipt.

1. **Pump screen.** Litres and total must be easy to read. Get the truck's number plate or cab in the picture.
2. **Dashboard.** The odometer number must be easy to read.
3. **Receipt**, if there is one.

Send them in one WhatsApp message with this line:

```
Fuel - AFO 0724 - Chris - Sun 18 Oct 07:40 - Odometer 377,950 - 24.3 L - $47.38 - filled to full
```

The odometer is the part that cannot be faked with an old photo: it has to be higher than the week before. A truck that did not move does not use fuel, and a photo from an earlier week shows a lower number.

## Monday check (10 minutes)

The weekly checker does this once a week, on Monday before work.

1. Open myfleet, then Fuel Log. Make sure every fill from the weekend is entered and the photos box is ticked.
2. Look at the weekly table. Each truck shows litres, cost, km driven and km per litre next to what is normal for that truck.
3. Read the result for each truck (next section) and the notes in the last column of the fill-up list.
4. Open the tracker for one truck. Check it stopped at the station around the time of the photo. Pick a different truck each week.
5. Click Copy WhatsApp summary and paste it in the group.
6. Write down anything that needs a conversation with a driver.

## What the results mean

A truck is marked OK, Check or Review each week. Normal km per litre is about 7.4 for the DAF and 9.6 for each Dyna, until the first four weeks give us the real figures.

| Result | It means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| OK | Within 15% of normal | Nothing |
| Check | 15% to 30% worse than normal, or photos missing, or a second fill within 12 hours, or an odd price | Ask the driver the same day, in a friendly way. Write the answer in the notes |
| Review | More than 30% worse than normal, or the odometer did not go up | Talk to the driver first. If it is not explained, or happens again, tell Dad |

One Check is normal. A heavy load, a long trip or traffic can cause it. Two Checks in a row on the same truck means look at that truck's trips on the tracker.

For the first four Sundays nobody is accused. We are finding out what is normal for each truck.

## Private use and long trips

Private fuel is paid by the family and never mixed with business fuel.

- **Family errands in a truck (for example the AFO).** Buy that fuel separately with family money. Enter it in Fuel Log as private and write PVT in the logbook. Private fuel never counts in the business costs or profit.
- **Cash hires.** Record the money as Cash hires under Expenses, so income is not missed.
- **Long or express trips.** Before leaving, post in the group: the truck, the place, the km, the litres needed and the amount. Litres needed = km divided by 7.4 for the DAF, or by 9.6 for a Dyna. The cash giver replies with approval. Fill to full before leaving and again on return, with the same photos.
- **Fuel money is for fuel only.** Any change goes back to the cash giver.

## Monthly look for Dad

Dad reads one short message at the end of each month and only decides two things.

The weekly checker sends:

- Litres and dollars spent on fuel for the month
- Km per litre for each truck, next to normal
- How many Check and Review results there were, and what was done
- Revenue, costs and profit from the Profitability page in myfleet

Dad decides:

1. What to do about any Review that stayed unexplained.
2. Whether the weekly fuel amounts need to change.

Everything else is handled by the weekly checker.

## What to tell the drivers

Say it in person, then post it in the group. Keep it calm.

> From this Sunday, fuel works differently. You will not need to ask for fuel money each time. Every Sunday each truck is filled to full, and you send the pump and odometer photos in the group. All the trucks have trackers, and everything is checked every Monday. This is for every truck and every driver, not for one person. If a number looks strange, we will ask you first. There are often good reasons, like a heavy load. Thank you for your help.

Before announcing, check what the drivers' agreements say about tracking and tell them in writing.
