# Trips, trailers, documents, fuel and maintenance

Part of the MyFleet manual QA suite. Conventions, priorities and the pass/fail procedure are in [../README.md](../README.md). Fixtures are in [../TEST_DATA.md](../TEST_DATA.md). Fill the **Result** column with PASS, FAIL, BLOCKED or N/A, and put the defect id in **Notes**. The same cases are in [../qa-tracker.csv](../qa-tracker.csv) for tracking.

Env column: **S** = run on staging/local only (it changes data or time-dependent state); **SP** = safe to run on production with a dedicated QA company.

## TRP: Trips and cross-border (18 cases)

Module "Cross-border trips" (Trucking > Trips, /del-trips). A trip is one whole load with its own agreed price (not rate x km). Statuses: planned, in_transit, delivered, closed, cancelled. A trip is cross-border automatically when its stops carry more than one distinct country code. Currencies: USD, ZAR, ZWG. Cost types: fuel, toll, zinara, border_fee, transit_fee, permit, agent_fee, parking, fine, allowance, repairs, other. Margin = price minus cost lines in the SAME currency as the trip; if any cost line is in another currency the margin is not calculated.

## FUE: Fuel log and weekly fuel check (15 cases)

Module "Fuel log" (/del-fuel-log). Company admins only (API 403 for managers). Procedure: docs/FUEL_PROCEDURE.md. Rules in services/fuelCheck.js. Check = 15% to 30% worse than normal, or photos missing, or a second fill within 12 hours, or an unusual price; Review = more than 30% worse, or the odometer did not go up. km per litre only when this fill AND the previous fill were both "filled to full". Expected km per litre: the truck's own value, else a built-in value for the registrations AFE5848 7.4, AAV4331 9.6, AFO0724 9.6, else by size (small 9.6, medium 7.4, large 5.0, horse 3.5), else 8. For the worked example set QAC3003's expectedKmPerLitre to 4.0 through the API.

## MNT: Maintenance, service plans and tyres (14 cases)

Module "Maintenance & tyres" (/del-maintenance). Open to all company users. Service plan due logic: by km (due soon when km left is at most the smaller of 1,000 or 10% of the interval; overdue when negative) and/or by days (due soon at 14 days or fewer; overdue when negative). Current odometer = highest reading known for the truck from job mileage-in, fuel fills, workshop records and tyre fitments.
