# Expenses, quotations, invoices, payments, PDFs and analytics

Part of the MyFleet manual QA suite. Conventions, priorities and the pass/fail procedure are in [../README.md](../README.md). Fixtures are in [../TEST_DATA.md](../TEST_DATA.md). Fill the **Result** column with PASS, FAIL, BLOCKED or N/A, and put the defect id in **Notes**. The same cases are in [../qa-tracker.csv](../qa-tracker.csv) for tracking.

Env column: **S** = run on staging/local only (it changes data or time-dependent state); **SP** = safe to run on production with a dedicated QA company.

## EXP: Expenses and profitability (15 cases)

Modules "Expenses & profitability": Expenses (/del-expenses) and Profitability (/del-profitability). Company admins only. Expense categories: fuel, private_fuel (tracked, never counted), driver_salary, owner_salary, family_salary, insurance, service, other. Extra income categories: cash_hire, other_income. Profitability for a year = job revenue + extra income - counted expenses. A fuel ESTIMATE for a truck and month is dropped when real fill-ups exist for that truck and month; business fill-ups are added as fuel expenses; private fills are shown separately. "Left in the business" salary is a cost but is added back for cash profit.

## QUO: Quotations (9 cases)

Module "Quotations" (Billing > Quotations, /del-quotations). Admin only. Numbered QUO-0001, QUO-0002 ... at creation. Valid-until defaults to 14 days after the quotation date. Statuses: draft, sent, accepted, rejected. Allowed moves: draft to sent; sent to accepted, rejected or draft; rejected to sent; accepted is final. Accepted and rejected quotations cannot be edited.
